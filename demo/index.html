GlyphProbe — local demo

A safe target that runs only in your browser. Pick how this "server" treats the input, insert probes into the field, click Reflect, then open the extension popup and click Scan page. The result should match the mode you picked.

Tip: start with No transform to confirm the probes are detected as unchanged, then switch to NFKC or Windows best-fit and reflect again to watch the detection light up.
